Mission Briefing & Background

  • Infiltration: You begin inside the overhead air duct above the 2nd-Floor Bathroom (classic 007 Facility style). Knock out the vent louver and drop into the stall.
  • Hostile Force: The facility is patrolled by authoritarian regime guards wearing White Armbands with Blue Sheriff Stars. Eliminate them quietly or slip past their patrol cones.
  • Optional Evidence (Risk vs. Score): 6 classified evidence items (war crimes dossiers & political manipulation wiretaps) are scattered across the 2F Offices, 2F/1F Science Labs, Control Room, and Warehouse. Grabbing evidence boosts your final score (+2,500 pts each), reduces your Evasiveness (-12% each), and deploys +2 extra patrolling guards per pickup!
  • Utility & Control Rooms: On Floor 1, plant a Smoke Bomb in the Utility Room HVAC to lure guards away from the exit, OR Hack the Control Room Terminal to unlock all doors & disable cameras (at risk of a hack backfire alarm!).

Controls & Mechanics

  • Move / Strafe: W A S D or Arrow Keys
  • Turn / Aim: Mouse (Click view to lock pointer) or Q / E & J / L
  • Fire / Use Gadget: Left Click or F
  • Interact (Doors, Evidence, Vent, Hack, Smoke Plant, Exit): E or click center prompt
  • Weapons: 1 PP7 Silenced | 2 KF7 Rifle | 3 Smoke Bomb | 4 Slap/Hack
  • Guard Small Talk: Lingering near guards shows 3D Speech Bubbles over their heads (auto-fading in gameplay). Press C anytime to enter Cutscene Mode where you press CONTINUE through dialogue.
